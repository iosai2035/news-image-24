Middle East · The Blockade

Iran Vows to Defy U.S. Naval Blockade as Gulf Tensions Deepen

Tehran says it will use all its tools against the blockade, as Washington’s envoys meet Qatar’s premier.
Two warships hold position in the Gulf on Tuesday, as Tehran vowed to use all its tools against the American blockade, first imposed on 13 April, lifted in June and reinstated on 14 July. — Illustration

Tehran said on Tuesday it would use all its tools to confront the American naval blockade. Iran’s foreign ministry spokesman, Esmaeil Baghaei, said the blockade would be met with every means at Iran’s disposal.

The blockade was first imposed on 13 April, lifted in June and reinstated on 14 July. In Washington, Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio met separately with Qatar’s prime minister, Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani.

The Qatari premier warned the Middle East could not afford another escalation between the United States and Iran.

Gaza · Targeted Strike

Israeli Strikes Kill Hamas Security Chief in Gaza

Israeli airstrikes on Tuesday killed Naeem Abu Nahel, the director of Hamas’s internal security apparatus in Gaza, and six others, including two police officers, medics and police said.

Strikes the day before killed at least three people in Gaza, among them a man working for the United Nations refugee agency.

Ukraine · Zaporizhzhia

Russian Strike Kills Two, Wounds 40 in Zaporizhzhia

An overnight Russian attack struck an apartment building in the city of Zaporizhzhia, killing two people and wounding 40, among them four children, the regional governor said.

Space · Nato Warning

NATO Warns Over a Possible Russian Nuclear Satellite

Reports say Russia could launch a nuclear-armed satellite before the end of 2026. Mark Rutte, the Nato secretary-general, said the alliance would defend against any threat in space.
